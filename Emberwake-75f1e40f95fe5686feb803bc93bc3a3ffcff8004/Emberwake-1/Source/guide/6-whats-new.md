# What's new

Newest first.

## The Ashfall, made again, and the weather

**The battle royale**

- **Everyone falls out of the sky with nothing**, over different parts of the island. No tools, no weapons, no kits, for Wakers and echoes alike (the pass gives titles now, not gear).
- **A much bigger island:** 3,500 paces, nine lands that run into one another, lakes, woods, bushes, boulders, named places and houses in the sky.
- **Dig anywhere.** The caves under it are new: winding tunnels, halls, sinkholes, and places worth finding. About 145 paces down the rock burns you; at 170 is the molten sea.
- **You make your weapons.** A third as many chests, two or three plain things in each, dropped where the chest stands. Weapons in chests are rare and never great ones.
- **Weapons are closer to each other.** Better metal helps without deciding the fight, and the easy ones (spears, tridents, battleaxes, bows) give up some bite.
- **The border waits for people to fall**, not for a clock.
- **Pacts can be left** (**P** twice, or **LEAVE PACT** in the menu), and echoes can be your allies.
- **Beasts** that can kill you, **sponsors' gifts**, **the Feast**, **the Marked One**, **wildfire and poison fog**, **the hounds**, and the faces of the fallen in the sky.
- Echoes gather, dig, craft, hide, run and make pacts as you do.
- Reeds twist into twine, dead bushes give a little fibre, and ropes, ladders and vines no longer snag your shoulder or catch you again when you leap off.
- **Fixed:** weapons you made couldn't be used, some houses couldn't be entered, bare hands couldn't dig, a ward wore away by itself.

**Everywhere**

- **Weather** in the Waking Lands and the Ashfall, with rare creatures of its own (see *How to play*).
- **More kinds of tree** (oak, willow, maple, pine, palm, mangrove), which sway in the wind.
- **You keep your momentum.** A blow or a dodge no longer stops you dead until you press a direction again.
- **Fire burns where it lands**, with real flames instead of a red streak.
- **Much smoother with many Wakers on screen**, fewer things left lying about, and an error in one frame no longer stops the game.

## Benches, trinkets, guardians and a bigger world

- **Ten benches, each with one job:** the Workbench, a new **Carpenter's Table**, the Anvil, a new **Armourer's Stand**, a new **Tinker's Table**, a new **Jeweller's Bench**, a new **Hearth Oven**, the Cauldron, a new **Offering Altar** and the Mason's Bench, plus the workshops for materials. Crafting by hand from your pack is still there.
- **The recipe book (B):** every bench, what it is for, how to build it and everything it makes. Every item says where it is made, and long bench lists have **SHOW** and **CAN MAKE** buttons.
- **About 90 new things to make:** trinkets, meals, furniture, storage, traps, lamps, gadgets and building blocks.
- **Trinkets** you wear in trinket slots (two to start, up to six), and **meals** that give a boon for a few minutes.
- **Things to place that do something:** beds that rest you (cosy with a table, a chair and a light), planters, beehives, fountains, braziers, crates, barrels and strongboxes, signposts on your map, a weapon rack, spike traps, snares, the ember ballista and a warning bell.
- **The guardians need offerings.** No guardian wakes by chance any more: each needs an offering made at the Offering Altar from things you gather and a sigil or relic from the guardian before it. The Iron Anvil needs a Warden's Sigil. Each guardian leaves a trophy trinket, and the first four open trinket slots. The Pale Shepherd only walks an Ember Moon you called.
- **Fighting styles you learn.** Each weapon family starts with its basics and teaches you more forms as you use it (FOOTWORK, FLOW, MASTERY). Attacks set each other up: sweeps knock down for a CRUSH, launches for a JUGGLE and a SPIKE, lunges open a foe for a BREAK, thrusts SKEWER a held foe, and light blows build RHYTHM. A perfect dodge opens four different counters, and the better weapons hide **arts** that you unlock by using them (hold the right mouse button).
- **A bigger world:** a wider view so your Waker is smaller on screen, a steadier walking pace, lands about twice as wide with taller hills, and creatures that come from beyond the edge of the screen. Worlds made before keep their old lands.
- **Playing together:** friends start with nothing on their first visit; hosting from Emberwake.html in a browser works as well as from the app (the game keeps the connection alive in a background tab, and catches up if the browser slows it); the title screen shows the version, and the game says clearly when two versions meet. This version can't play with older ones, so send your friends the new file.

## A tidy folder

- Everything is in one folder: the app, the HTML file, this guide, and **Source** with the game's code in clearly named parts.
- This guide, with pictures, replaces the long README.
- One command builds everything from the source (see **Source/README.md**), and the tests now run on any computer.

## Playing together, anywhere

- Works on any internet connection: more message boards (three on the web's own port), and the **relay** when a network won't allow a direct line. It switches over by itself if a line breaks, and back again when a direct line comes up.
- A friend whose internet drops out gets back in by themselves.
- A world keeps its game code from one time to the next, and the join screen remembers the last code.
- **GO TO** a friend, friends on the map and minimap, and arrows at the screen edge pointing to friends out of sight.
- **PLAY TOGETHER** shows how each friend is connected and how quick the line is.
- The world keeps running when someone switches to another window, and their Waker rests safely meanwhile (the Mac app keeps running when hidden too).
- Fixes: doors no longer shut on a friend standing in them; explosions hurt everyone nearby and hit creatures once; a newly placed campfire or kiln is the same for everyone; chests and fires are used one person at a time, so nothing is doubled or lost; a friend joining while others moved no longer sees a frozen, nameless Waker; closing the window tells friends at once.
- **FPS mode:** turn off ambient and particle effects in the pause menu for a simpler, smoother picture on slower computers. It does not change gameplay or multiplayer simulation.

## Game codes, easier packs and crafting

- **Game codes:** six letters and numbers instead of long codes to copy back and forth (the long codes are still there as a backup).
- Taking things out of your pack works properly: drag stacks, drag one out to drop it, shift-click between the hotbar and the pack, **G** to drop; chests have **TAKE ALL** and **STORE ALL**.
- **Crafting: SIMPLE**: click what you want and it's made, with each ingredient shown as what you have against what it needs. The station games are still there as **HANDS-ON**.
- A calmer pace: fewer and quieter messages, softer screen shake, plainer chests near the start, and slower mastery.
