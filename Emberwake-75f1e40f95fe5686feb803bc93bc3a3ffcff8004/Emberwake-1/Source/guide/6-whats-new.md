# What's new

Newest first.

## Faster fights

- **The quickening.** Blows struck on the glint now **quicken**: each crisp one after a hit makes the next come sooner, up to five steps (a thin bar under your embers). Five start **DRUMFIRE**, about a second when timing lets go and quick presses **stoke** every blow; end it with a heavy **CRASH**. One press too soon is only **HASTY** (out of time, a step slower); hammering is still **RUSHED**. Press while a blow throws you to **shake it off** sooner. Computer fighters play the same way, as far as their skill allows. Between people a blow that did not bite now lands soft, as it does on an echo, and a friend's blows on a hollow hit as hard as the host's. Letting go of the left button no longer drops a heavy blow you are charging with the right. Old and new versions cannot play together.

## Two versions made into one

This version joins two that had grown apart. One had the Trials, the four worlds past the floors and fighting by the beat. The other had accounts, touch controls and weather you can feel. Now they are one game.

**Kept as it was, from the entry below:** the home screen, the look, fighting by the beat, the Ashfall Trials with their perks, amps and relics, parties, traits, moods, seasons, curios and the worlds past the floors.

**Brought in from the other side:**

- **Accounts and hats.** The game opens on a sign-in board. Make an account (a name and a password, kept in this browser) and your worlds are yours alone. Worlds from before go to the first account. Pick a look and a **hat**, and change them later with **CHANGE LOOK**. See *Start here: Signing in*.
- **Phones and tablets.** Touch controls appear by themselves: a stick, **JUMP**, **DASH** (the dodge), **USE**, **LIGHT** and **HEAVY**, with buttons for the pack, the map, a quick heal, the menu and chat. The beat is the same under a thumb. See *Controls: On a phone or tablet*.
- **You keep your run.** Hold a direction through a blow or a dodge and you keep most of your pace instead of stopping dead. Hands off, you glide to a stop. A blow thrown from standing is exactly as it was.
- **Weather is felt.** Rain and snow slow you in the open and make the ground slick, snow and ash steal your breath, and creatures see less far in rain and fog. A roof, a ledge or a cave shelters you from all of it.
- **A thunderstorm has a wind.** It blows the way the rain leans: arrows and thrown things drift on it, and a run with it is quicker than one against it. Lightning likes a tall tree, and a struck tree often comes down.
- **Six creatures of the weather:** the puddling, the stormcaller, the galewisp, the mistwraith, the rimefang and the dustmaw. Each comes out only in its own weather and goes when it does.
- **Fire clings:** a flask broken against a wall or under a roof also burns there, in small moving flames, and one that bursts high in the air rains burning drops. Rain puts these small flames out sooner, and a bolt of lightning can start one. A flask's strip of burning ground is just as it was.
- **Eating takes a moment**, and a blow interrupts it (you keep the food).
- **Brews.** Quickstep at a cauldron, Emberheart at a **Brewer's Stand**, and at a **Moon Cauldron** the Doublesoul Draught, which calls a mirror of you to fight at your side. And a new meal, the Willow Omelet. See *How to play: Eating and brews*.
- **Two new lands** in worlds you forge from now on: the **Mosswood** (deep green, oaks, moss deer) and the **Willowmere** (reeds, still water, willows, willow hens). Worlds made before keep their land exactly as it was.
- **Worlds you already have keep their land.** A world made in the earlier version of this game (the one with accounts and hats) opens on exactly the land it was made with, its Mosswood and Willowmere included, so everything you built, dug and placed is where you left it. Such a world does not have the gates past the floors, the observatory or the other surface wonders: a world forged now has them all.
- **A third arena for the Ashfall: THE WILDS.** Thirty-two fall from the sky with nothing, over an island 3,500 paces wide with caves beneath it. Dig, gather, make your weapons, make pacts. It pays **souls** and **coins** for the **soul wheel** and the **shop**, where skins and hats are won and worn. See *The Ashfall Trials: A third arena*.
- **Armour at the Workbench.** Every piece of armour is now made at the Workbench, for fewer materials and with no anvil of its rank close by. An Armourer's Stand in an old world stays where it is.
- **FPS MODE** in the menu (**Esc**): no particles and a barer HUD, with an aiming cross, for a cleaner and smoother view. The game stays side-on. See *Controls: The menu*.
- **Ropes, ladders and vines** no longer snag your shoulder or catch you again when you leap off, in every world.
- **Dead bushes** give a little fibre as well as sticks, in every world.
- A frame that goes wrong no longer stops the game.
- **This version can't play with older ones, so send your friends the new files.**

**And new in this one: the echoes think.** They find a real way to where they are going (over walls, across gaps, up to a chest above the ground, round what they cannot climb, never into slag), run to somewhere safe instead of just away, pick the fights they can win, and keep the beat as well as their skill allows. The echoes of the Wilds find their way too, and dig one where there is none. See *The Ashfall Trials: The echoes*.

## Worlds with a character of their own, the Trials' own kit, parties, a new look

- **No two worlds alike.** Every world you forge now has **three traits** from its seed (one for its land, one for its life, one for its sky: *cragged, feral, twin moons*; *drowned, quiet days, long nights*...). The forge screen shows them, with a **ROLL** button for another seed. See *How to play: No two worlds alike*.
- **Lands have moods.** A moor may be burnt black or open meadow lit by glowcaps; the Reach has its Glass Spires and its White Quiet. Each mood has its own ground, plants, creatures and name.
- **A year of four seasons** (three days each: Kindling, Highsun, Emberfall, Ashrest), with **rain and snow**, stalks that turn with the season, and different rules in each.
- **Curios on the road:** milestones that point the way, a Waker's Rest that teaches a weapon, ember springs, the Singing Stones (a little puzzle in rhythm), buried caches, a hermit's notes, the Ribs.
- **Fighting goes further.** A heavy blow on a broken guard is a **SUNDER**. The blow after a clash is your **ANSWER**. Let a charged blow go on the flash for a **PERFECT RELEASE**. **Warded** foes carry shields that turn plain blows from the front. **Elder** creatures in some worlds. Mashing now costs stamina too. Each kind of weapon keeps the beat its own way.
- **Damage was rebalanced.** Kinds of weapon now sit in one band (the slowest hit hardest, the longest least, and none is twice another). **Tools no longer out-hit swords** in the late worlds: a tapped tool does half what a sword of its metal does, at every tier.
- **The Ashfall Trials have their own kit:** fourteen **perks** (choose one on the plinth and one each dawn), ten **amps** to fit to your weapons, six **relic** weapons, a second arena (**Ember Peak**, where slag rises instead of ash closing), match titles and a career record.
- **Parties.** Make a party with friends, then take the whole party into someone else's open match, or open yours to others. Friends land on one team. See *The Ashfall Trials: Parties*.
- **A new look.** Fires, pods, embers and blades **glow**. Each land, mood, season and kind of weather tints the air. **Mist** in the morning. Every world has **its own stars and constellations**, stars fall, the low sun throws rays, some worlds have a second moon. Your Waker trails **embers at a run**, brighter in tempo; a blow in time leaves a gold trail, a crisp one a white one, a rushed one a dull one; the view jolts with a hit. **FX: PLAIN** in the menu turns the costly parts off.
- Worlds made before this keep their land exactly as it was (no traits, moods or curios), and get the seasons, the weather and the new look. **This version can't play with older ones, so send your friends the new files.**

## Fighting by the beat, the Ashfall Trials, four new worlds, a new home screen

- **Fighting was rebuilt around timing.** Your weapon glints after each blow: strike on the glint and the chain goes on; strike before it and the blow is RUSHED (weak, slow, open). Blows in time build **tempo** (five embers), which a heavy blow spends, up to a CRESCENDO. Clean hits (the sweet spot of each weapon), CLASHES (your swing meeting theirs), guards that BREAK, and being CAUGHT in your wind-up all matter now. Mashing the button loses to a steady hand, and holding it down no longer attacks. Echoes and rivals fight by the same rules. See *How to play: Fighting*.
- **The battle royale is now the Ashfall Trials:** a survival match of up to fourteen minutes. Plinths round the Hearth and a horn; hunger and berries; scrap and camp anvils; the crowd's favor and sponsor gifts; three nights with Hollow Ones and the roll of the fallen; two supply drops; a feast; and the ashfall closing on the Hearth. Play **solo, duos or trios**, and rekindle a fallen teammate.
- **Four new worlds past the three floors:** the Long Winter, the Stopped Hour, the Stormhead and the Outside, each with three lands, its own danger, a better forge, its own steel and weapons, new arts, and a guardian. Every guardian's fragments forge the **Dawnring**, which calls the last guardian of all. The story now has an ending.
- **The surface matters:** sunstone by day, skyiron from craters, sunbloom and moonpetal, skyfeathers, sun shrines, observatories, thunderstorms, lightning rods and storm glass. The four gates are all above ground.
- **Guardians are harder to wake and harder to beat:** each offering needs things from several worlds and from the surface; guardians have more health, a guard, and a rage. Lands are wider, and forts and castles are rarer.
- **A new home screen:** three boards (ALONE, WITH FRIENDS, THE ARENAS), a CONTINUE button for your last world, sound, difficulty and controls on the front page, and HOW TO PLAY.
- **The FFA Arcade has ten combat modes**, each with its own arena, weapon kit and saved stats. Quick play starts with three echo bots; private matches use a join code. Crystal PvP resets its arena every ten minutes.
- **Playing together is steadier:** when two players change the same spot at the same moment the host now settles it for everyone (this was the cause of worlds drifting apart); friends see a guardian's lightning and fire pillars. This version can't play with older ones, so send your friends the new files.

## Benches, trinkets, guardians and a bigger world

- **Ten benches, each with one job:** the Workbench, a new **Carpenter's Table**, the Anvil, a new **Armourer's Stand**, a new **Tinker's Table**, a new **Jeweller's Bench**, a new **Hearth Oven**, the Cauldron, a new **Offering Altar** and the Mason's Bench, plus the workshops for materials. Crafting by hand from your pack is still there.
- **The recipe book (B):** every bench, what it is for, how to build it and everything it makes. Every item says where it is made, and long bench lists have **SHOW** and **CAN MAKE** buttons.
- **About 90 new things to make:** trinkets, meals, furniture, storage, traps, lamps, gadgets and building blocks.
- **Trinkets** you wear in trinket slots (two to start, up to six), and **meals** that give a boon for a few minutes.
- **Things to place that do something:** beds that rest you (cosy with a table, a chair and a light), planters, beehives, fountains, braziers, crates, barrels and strongboxes, signposts on your map, a weapon rack, spike traps, snares, the ember ballista and a warning bell.
- **The guardians need offerings.** No guardian wakes by chance any more: each needs an offering made at the Offering Altar from things you gather and a sigil or relic from the guardian before it. The Iron Anvil needs a Warden's Sigil. Each guardian leaves a trophy trinket, and the first four open trinket slots. The Pale Shepherd only walks an Ember Moon you called.
- **Fighting styles you learn.** Each weapon family starts with its basics and teaches you more forms as you use it (FOOTWORK, FLOW, MASTERY). Attacks set each other up: sweeps knock down for a CRUSH, launches for a JUGGLE and a SPIKE, lunges open a foe for a BREAK, thrusts SKEWER a held foe, and light blows build RHYTHM. A perfect dodge opens four different counters, and the better weapons hide **arts** that you unlock by using them (hold the right mouse button).
- **A bigger world:** a wider view so your Waker is smaller on screen, a steadier walking pace, lands about twice as wide with taller hills, and creatures that come from beyond the edge of the screen. Worlds made before keep their old lands.
- **Playing together:** friends start with nothing on their first visit; hosting from Emberwake.html in a browser works as well as from the app (the game keeps the connection alive in a background tab, and catches up if the browser slows it); the title screen shows the version, and the game says clearly when two versions meet. This version can't play with older ones, so send your friends the new file.

## A tidy folder

- This guide, with pictures, replaces the long README.
- One command builds everything from the source (see **Source/README.md**), and the tests now run on any computer.

## Playing together, anywhere

- Works on any internet connection: more message boards (three on the web's own port), and the **relay** when a network won't allow a direct line. It switches over by itself if a line breaks, and back again when a direct line comes up.
- A friend whose internet drops out gets back in by themselves.
- A world keeps its game code from one time to the next, and the join screen remembers the last code.
- **GO TO** a friend, friends on the map and minimap, and arrows at the screen edge pointing to friends out of sight.
- **PLAY TOGETHER** shows how each friend is connected and how quick the line is.
- The world keeps running when someone switches to another window, and their Waker rests safely meanwhile (the Mac app keeps running when hidden too).
- Fixes: doors no longer shut on a friend standing in them; explosions hurt everyone nearby and hit creatures once; a newly placed campfire or kiln is the same for everyone; chests and fires are used one person at a time, so nothing is doubled or lost; a friend joining while others moved no longer sees a frozen, nameless Waker; closing the window tells friends at once.

## Game codes, easier packs and crafting

- **Game codes:** six letters and numbers instead of long codes to copy back and forth (the long codes are still there as a backup).
- Taking things out of your pack works properly: drag stacks, drag one out to drop it, shift-click between the hotbar and the pack, **G** to drop; chests have **TAKE ALL** and **STORE ALL**.
- **Crafting: SIMPLE**: click what you want and it's made, with each ingredient shown as what you have against what it needs. The station games are still there as **HANDS-ON**.
- A calmer pace: fewer and quieter messages, softer screen shake, plainer chests near the start, and slower mastery.
